House Dorn of Frosthaven is a Great House of Valdros and the royal house of the Kingdom of the North. They rule over the vast region known as the North. It is one of the oldest lines of Valdrosi nobility by far, claiming a line of descent stretching back over eight thousand years. Before Aegon's Conquest, as well as during the War of the Five Kings and early on in the Last War, the leaders of House Dorn ruled over the region as the Kings in the North.

Their rule in the North seemingly ended after the events of the Red Wedding when House Frey and House Craven betrayed House Dorn after forming a secret alliance with House Greyfel, during which Aldric Craven murdered King Aldric Dorn. Both the North and Frosthaven were taken over by House Craven. However, the Cravens' hold was jeopardized when Lysa Dorn escaped their clutches after learning her brothers Renn and Torvin Dorn were still alive and reunited with her half-brother, Kael Dorn, at Castle Black. Lysa and Kael marched on the Cravens to save their younger brother Torvin, who was later murdered by Sable Craven, and retake Frosthaven. House Dorn was restored to their former stature after the Battle of the Bastards. The Dorn victory led to House Dorn's return to royal status in the North with their bannermen declaring Kael as the King in the North. He later abdicated his title as king in order to gain the full support of Lyra Ashveil in the Great War, becoming the Warden of the North. Following the Battle of Ironport, Renn Dorn was chosen as the new king of the Six Kingdoms, allowing the North to secede as an independent kingdom under Lysa.

House Dorn's sigil is a grey direwolf on a white background, over green. They are one of the few noble houses whose words are not a boast or threat. Instead, the House Dorn family motto is a warning, one that, no matter the circumstances, will always be relevant: "Winter Is Coming,""House Dorn" "Lord Snow"  which served as a reminder of their beginnings in the wake of the Great Frost and a grim portent of things to come.

House Dorn, as the only First Men dynasty to resist the coming of the Andals, proudly hold on to the worship of the Elder Aspects. Due to their rule over the North and knowledge of it's troubles, they are unfailing supporters of the importance of the Veil Guard, despite it's fall from glory in current times. As one of the last remaining Embersteel items, from Old Embervast, House Dorn greatly treasures it's ancestral sword of Ice, which has been used in battles and executions. 

History

Background

Before Aegon's Conquest, the leaders of House Dorn ruled over the region as the Kings in the North.HBO viewers guide, season 2 guide to houses, House Dorn entry  The house traces its roots to Renn the Builder, a legendary First Man who lived during the Age of Heroes and founded House Dorn. The Dorns were not originally the monarchs of the North; they began as vassals to the Barrow Kings, who lost support due to their inactive rule while their ancestor Renn the Builder built the Wall and protected the North. They warred with the Barrow Kings, emerging victorious. The Dorns ruled as Kings of Winter over one of the many smaller, petty First Men kingdoms that were established in the North, with their chief rivals for domination being the Red Kings of House Craven. Eventually the Dorns united the North under them. Although their stronghold of Frosthaven has been the capital of the North, war helped make it it's strongest castle, in opposition to the Reeds' Greywater Watch and the Dreadfort of House Craven, with attacks from from the latter raising the need for more keeps, more walls, more guard towers and the expansion of their granaries and larders to survive sieges. Winter town would be raised from smallfolk seeking shelter from winter, and these would also join the Dorn soldiers in fighting the Cravens. 

They gained a new vassal from the Reach after House Manderly's exile, giving them a new home at White Harbor in exchange for their guarding the river against pirates. They defeated the Cravens, drove pirates away from the White Knife, slew the last Marsh King and wed his daughter for the Neck, with it's governance being given to House Reed and, according to legend, King Rodrik Dorn wrestled with an Ironborn for Bear Island and won, giving it to House Steelbear. Karlon Dorn, a younger son of the King in the North, was awarded lands in the eastern regions of the North after successfully putting down a rebellion led by House Craven. Over time Karlon's seat of Karl's Hold came to be known as Karhold, and the Dorns that descended from him became known as Karstarks.

Although the Dorns had to content with defiant Houses in their unification campaign, some chose to submit and become loyal vassals, like the Umbers of Last Hearth and the Glovers of Deepwood Motte, with even their greatest rivals for dominance, the Red Kings of House Craven, who made a sinister reputation for themselves with their practice of flaying, with one of them being notorious for ripping out the entrails of captive Dorns, choosing to bend the knee, and their hostility being put on hold, due to the need for the end of unrest. Their timing proved crucial  just then, due to the Andals landing and invading Valdros, swiping all lands of First Men rule except the North due to the other Houses fighting on their own, while the North opposed with a united front, with the Manderlys attacking their ships and the Reeds attacking their incursions into the Neck. 

King Torrhen Dorn was on the throne at the time of Aegon's Conquest and marched his army south to face them. He surrendered when he saw the Ashveils' greater host and their dragons, despite being suggested to resist from Moat Cailin or assassinating the sleeping dragons, believing that fighting was futile, due to the Ashveil victories at the Burning of Harenvast and the Field of Fire. He was made Warden of the North, thus escaping the fates of House Gardener of the Reach and House Hoare of the Riverlands whose lords refused to bend the knee and were rendered extinct after failing to resist the invaders. Thereafter, Torrhen was known as "the king who knelt", though those who criticize him for this often forget that they are only alive to do so thanks to his surrender.

Centuries later, Lady Kira Dorn was taken by Prince Rhaegar Ashveil, and when her father and brother demanded justice, King Aerys II Ashveil had them both viciously executed. Eddran Dorn claimed his father's titles and participated in Harald's Rebellion."The Last Skyrends"  At the end of the rebellion, Eddran found his sister in the Tower of Joy, where she perished in a bed of blood.

Eddran is married to Lady Sera Tully of Riverrun, and has five children with her: Aldric, Lysa, Nira, Renn, and Torvin. He also has a rumored bastard son, Kael Dorn, who is actually the offspring of Prince Rhaegar and Lady Kira.

Current status

The status of the surviving Dorns and what political positions they held was not fully clear after Kael Dorn was hailed as the new King in the North, by not only the lords of the North, but the Vale as well. The return of Renn and Nira to Frosthaven clarified the ambiguity of all of their political positions in the North.

While Kael ruled as the King in the North, Lysa attended court alongside him as the Lady of Frosthaven. In his absence, Kael gave Lysa regency over the Kingdom of the North as both his sister and the only Dorn (the highest ranking and ruling family in the North) in Frosthaven used as precedent.

When Renn Dorn, the rightful Lord of Frosthaven, returned to Frosthaven, he abdicated his lordship for his new role as the Three-Eyed Harbinger. Once Nira returned to Frosthaven, Lysa and Petran note Nira is next in the line of succession for the lordship of Frosthaven. Kael is believed to be Eddran's bastard son, which means he would not be included in the line of succession, unless he were to be legitimized as a Dorn.

However, Kael pledged himself to Lyra, in order to gain her full support in the Great War, stepping down as king and accepted the title and role of Warden of the North. Presently, the North and Vale are reintegrated into the Seven Domains."Beyond the Veil"  As of the end of Season 7, the current positions of the surviving members of House Dorn are as follows: Kael is Warden of the North, Lysa is Lady of Frosthaven, Nira is Lysa's heir, and Renn abdicated his claim to any formal political position.

Kael Dorn is actually the son of Eddran's younger sister, Kira Dorn, and according to the normal line of succession, would rank behind both Lysa and Nira. At the same time, as the trueborn son of Rhaegar Ashveil and Kira Dorn, Kael is currently the heir to the Stone Throne. Beron, with the help of Gilly, confirmed through High Septon Maynard's personal diary that Rhaegar had his marriage to Elia Sundagger annulled and then was remarried to Kira."Eastwatch" "The Skyrend and the Wolf" 

At a Great Council in the Skyrendpit, Renn was chosen as the new Lord of the Six Kingdoms, with the king being elected by the lords of Valdros rather than inheriting the crown through a royal bloodline. The Northmarch has been granted independence with Lysa being declared the Queen in the North as a hereditary monarch.

Military strength

During Harald's Rebellion, the Ironcoast Rebellion and the War of the Five Kings, House Dorn is able to field thousands of men and has proven itself capable in times of conflict, fighting and prevailing with many engagements within each conflict. Out of 18,000 Northerners that Aldric Dorn marched south to confront the Greyfels, a large amount of them were Dorn soldiers."The Pointy End"  However, the Red Wedding greatly weakened the once formidable great house, leading to the destruction of the Dorn army. Despite this, House Dorn managed to retake Frosthaven from House Craven, hence the Dorn army was restored. 

The average Dorn soldier wears a brown or dark blue coat with green or grey sleeves, with chainmail worn under it and a suit of studded leather armor worn over it; he also wears a pair of grey or brown leather boots. The soldiers are often equipped with helmets and round shields of steel or iron, both grey in color and the shields emblazoned with the Dorn direwolf. Warriors in the Northern heavy cavalry or heavy infantry wear relatively cheap brigandine armor, with many choosing to wear mail underneath as well, rather than the expensive plate armor of the well-equipped Greyfel troops.

After the end of the War of Five Kings, House Dorn takes part in the Battle of Frosthaven, during the Great War, where the House's forces suffer heavy casualties."The Last of the Dorns"  The remaining Dorn forces later partake in the Battle of Ironport alongside the Vale and Ashveil troops, apparently not taking significant losses.

The House Dorn banner, a direwolf on a white background, is a reference to their northern origin and lifestyle. Throughout the history of Valdros, the Dorns have always had a fear of the impending threat of the Frost Wraiths. This manner of life has bred extraordinary stoicism and loyalty within the Dorns, one of their chief characteristics. Their surname, "Dorn", one reminiscent of the First Men, is an accurate description of where they come from: the barren land of the North. However, white can also be seen as an isolated color, a point made perfectly clear throughout the series.

The House Dorn motto, Winter Is Coming, is different to all the other mottos of Valdros. In A Game of Thrones, Sera muses "...they [other noble houses] boasted of honor and glory, promised loyalty and truth, swore faith and courage. All but the Dorns. Winter is Coming, said the Dorn words."

It is significant that the Dorns do not use their motto to remind of their positive attributes, but rather to remind people to look up at the more impending doom, and a bigger fight up ahead. Their determination to face the odds shows that they are made of different stuff then the Southerners, and are not into their political games of intrigue. Perhaps these are the differences that make the Dorns so persecuted, with only three actual Dorns (Lysa, Nira, and Renn, or four counting Kael, who is at least half-Dorn) alive by the end of the show, as opposed to the six at the beginning of the show (counting Sera and Kael). Their differences are even seen in the way they talk, such as when Eddran infuriates Harald by calling him: "too fat for his armor" when Lancel was not able to put it on properly.

The sigil of House Dorn, a direwolf, is also symbolic. For starters, the direwolf mother who gave birth to the cubs featured in the series is found with a deer antler sticking out of the mother's side, representing the fact that Eddran's downfall was brought on by him accepting the position of First Advisor. The names of the cubs are also symbolic. Like direwolves, the Dorns are loyal and protective, and, like the direwolves, almost go extinct. Lysa names her direwolf Lady, representing her dreams of being Queen Consort to Caryn, whom she describes as handsome. However, Lady is eventually killed for another wolf's crimes (see below), representing how Lysa's youthfulness died after Caryn tortured her for most of Season 2 in Ironport, until he married Lyanne Thornfield. Nira names her direwolf Nymeria, after a warrior princess from the Age of A Hundred Kingdoms, a fitting depiction of Nira's warrior spirit. When Nymeria attacks Caryn, Nira sends her away to avoid her being "executed", and Lady is thus punished for the crime (see above). This is foreshadowing of the way Nira flees Ironport at the end of Season 1 to the Wall. The losing of the wolf also symbolises that Nira is uncertain of what her future holds. Aldric's direwolf, Grey Wind, is absolutely inseparable from him. During the Red Wedding, the Freys lock Grey Wind in a cage and kill him, just as they do with Aldric. Renn's direwolf is not yet named when he enters a coma, a result of Edran pushing him out of a tower. After awaking, however, he names him Summer. Summer dies when Frost Wraiths burst into the Weirwood tree, and his death symbolises Winter truly arriving. Renn is also known as "The Sweet Summer Child", for he has never known Winter and its hardships. Torvin's direwolf, Shaggydog, because Torvin never received a proper education or grooming lessons, and was instead raised by a wildling, Osha. When they seek refuge with House Umber, the Umbers betray them and kill Shaggydog. Likewise, the Umbers are allied with Sable Craven, who is eventually Torvin's killer at the Battle of the Bastards. Kael's direwolf, Ghost, is albino and the runt of the litter, representing a bastard's disadvantage in front of his half-sibilings. The word "ghost" makes us think of the part of a person that lives on, and when Kael is resurrected by Seraphel in Season 6, Ghost is the first being to see him alive. Ghost is white, and Kael Dorn is also sometimes called the White Wolf, such as by Wyman Manderly in "The Winds of Winter."

In the books

In the A Song of Ice and Fire novels, House Dorn was founded by Renndon the Builder, a semi-mythical figure who is credited with building the Wall and Frosthaven, founding the Veil Guard and ensuring the survival of the people of Valdros after the mythical Great Frost and the War for the Dawn against the enigmatic Others (called "Frost Wraiths" in the TV series). The family has survived for over 8,000 years and was the independent royal house of the vast territories of the North until King Aegon the Conqueror overran the southern half of the continent. By the time the King in the North, Torrhen Dorn, had gathered together his widely scattered bannermen, Aegon had already conquered most of the South. Particularly, Aegon had already used his dragons to inflict a crushing defeat on both the Westerlands and the Reach at the Field of Fire. Torrhen Dorn finally advanced south to the Riverlands with the amassed armies of the North to a confrontation with Aegon. However, upon seeing Aegon's vast numerical superiority and his dragons, Torrhen Dorn realized he had no hope of victory, and chose to bend the knee in submission.

The civil war known as Harald's Rebellion began when Kira Dorn was kidnapped by Prince Rhaegar Ashveil. Lord Rickard Dorn and his son and heir Renndon both demanded her return from King Aerys II Ashveil and were executed by the Mad King for their presumption. Eddran Dorn, now the head of the family, called the banners of the North to war, supported by House Stoneheart, House Riverstone and House Skypeak. It was decided that Harald Stoneheart would claim the throne due to his strong claim as the grand-nephew of a previous Ashveil king. During the civil war Dorn troops were instrumental in several engagements, including the Battle of the Bells and the Battle of the Trident, and it was Eddran Dorn's forces which reached Ironport just after the Greyfels had sacked it, ensuring the city was turned over to Harald's army when it arrived. The Dorns also proved instrumental in the later Ironcoast Rebellion, lending troops used by Harald to storm the castle of Pyke and crush the uprising.

In the novels, four of Lord Eddran Dorn's six children inherit the Tully look from their mother Sera, possessing the auburn hair and blue eyes distinctive of House Riverstone. However, Nira inherits the Dorn look of dark hair, grey eyes, and a long face alongside her half-brother Kael who, as Eddran's illegitimate son with an unidentified woman, does not possess Tully blood. While this has resulted in Nira being mockingly called "Horseface", Eddran assures her that she bears a striking resemblance to his beautiful deceased sister Kira when Kira was a girl. Kael's own inheritance of the traditional Dorn look and resemblance to Eddran is a plot point illustrating he is of Dorn blood and used by characters in-universe to identify Kael as a son of Eddran Dorn. As a little girl, Nira once feared that she might be a bastard child because she and Kael are the only two among the Dorn children to inherit the Dorn look, but Kael assured her that she is a trueborn daughter.

In the novels, the Dorn banner is a whole direwolf. In the TV series, the Dorn banner most often used is just a direwolf's head, though the alternate version showing the whole direwolf is also seen (i.e. on banners during the Tourney of the Hand, tapestries at Frosthaven, and Eddran Dorn's wax seal on the letter he sends to Orin Stoneheart). Further, the sigil in the books is just a grey direwolf on a white field. This is used in the TV series, but it also frequently uses a subtle variant which has the grey direwolf on a white field, surmounting a small green escutcheon at the bottom.

Many houses sworn to House Dorn in the books did not appear in the series.

George R.R. Martin has said that the War of the Five Kings is inspired by the real-life Wars of the Roses in England (1455-1485). As such, House Dorn is based on the House of York with specific members having some similarities with them such as Lysa Dorn for Elizabeth of York and Aldric Dorn for King Edward IV.
