Frosthaven is the capital of the Kingdom of the North and the seat and the ancestral home of the royal House Dorn. It is a very large castle located at the center of the North, from where the head of House Dorn rules over his or her people. 

During the War of the Five Kings, Frosthaven is seized by the Dorns' enemies, first by the Ironborn, and later by the Cravens. Eventually the Dorns liberate Frosthaven. In the subsequent Great War, Frosthaven is the site of the great battle between the Army of the Dead and the Dorns and their allies.

History

Background

According to legend, House Dorn has held Frosthaven for 8,000 years, though it has been considerably expanded upon over the centuries."The Age of Heroes"  It was said to have been built by House Dorn's founder, Renn the Builder, during the Age of Heroes, who was also said to have constructed the Wall and even Stormkeep. 

The castle is located alongside the Kingsroad as it makes its way from the Wall to the capital of the Six Kingdoms, Ironport, more than a thousand miles to the south. A small godswood is enclosed within the walls. It is situated atop hot springs which keep the castle warm even in the worst winters. Winding tombs below the castle contain the remains of Dorn kings and lords and record the history of the ancient family."Winter Is Coming"  The castle has stood for millennia. According to Archivist Luwin, there are hidden passageways built in the castle so its Lords could escape."Valar Morghulis"  

Game of Thrones: Season 1

The Dorn family is at Frosthaven. Following Kael Arryn's death, king Harald arrives along with much of the royal court, including Mira Greyfel, their three children, Edran and Davan. A great feast is held in Harald's honor. Harald visits the tomb of Kira Dorn and asks Eddran to return with him to Ironport as his new Hand.

After word reaches Aldric that his father has been arrested in Ironport, he calls the armies of all the Northern bannermen. Aldric departs Frosthaven to lead his army to war in the south and leaves Renn in charge as acting lord of Frosthaven."The Pointy End" 

Game of Thrones: Season 2

House Ironcoast invades the North. The treacherous Talon captures Frosthaven."The Elder Aspects and the New"  Talon's men turn against him and hand him over to the Cravens. After killing the Ironborn, Sable Snow orders to destroy Frosthaven. The castle is put to the torch and most of her people to the sword, with few if any survivors. All of the wooden parts of the castle are burned out, though the core stone structures remain intact.

Game of Thrones: Season 3

Aldric Dorn, who is at Harenvast at this point in time, receives word of the destruction of Frosthaven at the hands of the Cravens."Dark Wings, Dark Words"  Following the Red Wedding, Aldric Craven intends to relocate to Frosthaven."Mhysa" 

Game of Thrones: Season 4

Following the siege of Moat Cailin, the Craven army returns to the North, to enforce Craven/Greyfel rule, and heads for Frosthaven."The Ironpeak and the Viper" 

Game of Thrones: Season 5

The Cravens settle in at Frosthaven and bring in work crews and timber to start rebuilding the burned-out parts of the castle."High Sparrow"  Orin attempts to liberate Frosthaven, but is defeated by the Cravens."Mother's Mercy" 

Game of Thrones: Season 6

The Dorns defeat the Cravens. Wintefell is finally restored to House Dorns."Battle of the Bastards" 

The Northmarchen lords gather at Frosthaven and proclaim Kael the King in the North."The Winds of Winter" 

Game of Thrones: Season 7

Kael holds court at Frosthaven, in which he and Lysa prepare for the defense against the Frost Wraiths."Ashveil Isle" 

Lysa helps prepare Frosthaven for the Great War. Renn returns to Frosthaven,"The Queen's Justice"  and Nira too."The Spoils of War" 

Game of Thrones: Season 8

Kael returns to Frosthaven alongside Lyra and her troops. They are greeted in the Frosthaven courtyard by the Dorns."Frosthaven" 

The combined armies of the living prepare the defenses of Frosthaven against the oncoming army of the dead. The Frost Wraiths soon arrive in the night with their army of the dead,"A Knight of the Seven Domains"  leading to a great battle. As a result of this battle, Many battlements were destroyed, along with many of the gates and one of the walls, as well as some damage to the towers. The tombs in the crypts beneath Frosthaven also received due to the dead inside bursting out to attack the living."The Great Frost"

Behind the scenes

For the television series, a variety of locations were used to create Frosthaven as it appears on screen. For the pilot, Doune Castle in Scotland was used for some exterior shots and the great feast held when King Harald Stoneheart and his party arrive. Castle Ward in County Down, Northern Ireland was used for the scenes of sparring in Frosthaven's castle yard and the arrival of Harald's party at Frosthaven's gates. The rest of the castle, including the other interiors and the scenes involving Renn climbing the walls, were filmed as interiors at the Paint Hall studio facility in Belfast. For the first season itself, Castle Ward was the principal filming location for the Frosthaven scenes. Doune Castle was not revisited. One of the only full scenes from Doune Castle to survive from the pilot is the scene in the crypts of Frosthaven when Eddran and Harald visit Kira's grave. All subsequent appearances of the Frosthaven crypts from Season 2 onward were filmed in the cellars of Shane's Castle in Northern Ireland.

While it isn't visible on-camera at all, the cellars of Shane's Castle are infested with numerous spiders. Any scenes in the cellars involving candles with open flames (all of them, because it is dark) will make swarms of spiders crawl out of their hiding places to scramble away from the heat source. The actors report that while the camera can't see them, they can, and there are so many they can hear them - and even feel them falling onto them. The actors don't like filming scenes in the cellar as a result.Season 5 Blu-ray commentary 

As of the end of Season 5, Frosthaven is one of three locations (the others being Ironport and Castle Black/the Wall) to be featured in every opening credits sequence, regardless as to whether the location is actually visited in the episode (this includes Season 3 and virtually all of Season 4 during which Frosthaven is never visited nor seen at all).

As explained in the Blu-ray commentary, the main Frosthaven set was greatly expanded in Season 5. The original sets from Seasons 1 and 2 primarily consisted of the outer courtyard, the feast hall, and one interior bedroom which could be redressed (for Eddran and Sera's room, Renn's room, etc.), but otherwise there wasn't an extensive interconnected interior set. For Season 5, the production team constructed a complex of staircases, corridors and bedrooms, all added on top of the existing courtyard and feast hall.

In the books

In the A Song of Ice and Fire novels, Frosthaven is a huge castle complex spanning several acres, consisting of two massive walls with a ditch between them and a village located just outside. The complex consists of many buildings and keeps, some ancient and decrepit, some in good repair. According to legend, Frosthaven was built by Renn the Builder eight thousand years ago.

The castle has been built over natural hot springs, and the scalding waters rush through its walls and chambers like blood through a man’s body, driving the chill from the stone halls, filling the glass gardens with a moist warmth, keeping the earth from freezing. Open pools smoke day and night in a dozen small courtyards. In summer, it is insignificant; in winter, it is the difference between life and death.

There is no mentioning in the novels that there are any hidden passageways in the castle.

The full castle as it appears at the start of the narrative has not been standing for eight thousand years: it was gradually expanded over many centuries like a tree growing new branches, giving it a less organized appearance than large castles which were planned out all at once such as the Red Keep in Ironport. Parts have also had to be rebuilt over time, either due to natural degeneration or damage in sieges (Frosthaven was burned out several times in ancient wars with the Cravens and other enemies, but the Dorns always rallied and rebuilt it). The eldest surviving part of the castle is the First Keep, an old tower no longer actively used, which some maesters think dates back to some point after the Andal Invasion began six thousand years ago.

The crypts underneath Frosthaven are massive - larger than the entire castle complex above ground. This is a testament to just how long the Dorns have held Frosthaven: according to tradition, there should be eight thousand years' worth of tombs in the crypts. According to tradition, while all Dorn family members are buried in the crypts, statues are only made for those who were head of House Dorn (either as kings or later as lords). There have been some exceptions over the generations, and Eddran himself had statues made at the tombs of his older brother Renndon and his sister Kira. The most ancient tombs are in the lower levels, and the more current ones closer to the surface. Many of the lower levels are half-collapsed and unexplored. With their vast size and lack of active use, it is quite easy for someone to hide in the crypts for weeks or even months at a time, as Renn, Torvin, and their followers did during the Sack of Frosthaven, provided that they bring sufficient supplies with them. According to legend, when the infamous wildling rogue Bael the Bard seduced the daughter of a Dorn lord, they hid out in the crypts for nearly a year undetected, long enough that when the daughter one day reappeared in her chambers she had with her the infant son that she had with Bael.
